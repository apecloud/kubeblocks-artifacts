# Panel 3 v4 Prompt

Use case: comic panel base
Asset type: 16:9 text-free comic panel

Primary request: Minimal hand-drawn comic scene on a white background. A small municipal dispatch tower object with a blank rectangular screen is shown for the first time. The tower has no face, arms, or legs. Nearby is a records archive building split into four simple rooms. One room is being gently updated, shown by a small open service panel and a tiny orange circular arrow. The other rooms are still open, lit, and usable, with shelves of folders visible and one tiny person calmly taking a folder. An anxious but dignified archivist peeks from an open door, trying to look calm. A small black blob character with white dot eyes stands near the tower, deadpan.

Style: pure white background, minimalist black wobbly pen line art, sparse composition, lots of empty space, Ian Xiaohei-like editorial sketch feeling.

Composition: horizontal 16:9. Dispatch tower and blank screen on the left. Archive rooms on the right. Leave clear blank space around the tower screen and above the characters for later local text overlay.

Color: mostly black line art, muted blue-gray for open archive rooms, tiny restrained orange accent only on the one room being updated. No red, no disaster lighting.

Text: no readable text, no pseudo text, no letters, no numbers anywhere. All screens, signs, folders, and papers must be blank.

Avoid: logos, branding, speech bubbles, caption boxes, app screenshots, dense dashboard UI, fake text, malformed letters, watermarks, faces or limbs on the tower, a fully closed archive, dark outage scene, alarm scene.
