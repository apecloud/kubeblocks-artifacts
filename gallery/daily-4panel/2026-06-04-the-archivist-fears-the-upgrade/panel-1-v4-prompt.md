# Panel 1 v4 Prompt

Use case: comic-panel-base
Asset type: 16:9 comic panel base for a four-panel series

Primary request: Generate a text-free base illustration for Episode 3 Panel 1 of a minimalist Xiaohei-style comic. Scene: the entrance of a dignified database archive building, like a small city archive/library facility representing PostgreSQL. A careful archivist character stands by the entrance with a serious, rule-following posture, holding a ledger or checklist. A small blank notice board/sign is mounted near the door for later text overlay. A small solid-black Xiaohei creature with white dot eyes may stand nearby observing quietly, but not doing the main action. No control tower, no brand logo, no KubeBlocks text.

Style/medium: pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly pen lines, absurd editorial sketch style inspired by Ian Xiaohei illustrations. Lots of empty white space.

Composition/framing: horizontal 16:9, archive entrance centered-left, blank notice board near upper-left/door area, archivist near doorway, open whitespace above/right for later dialogue overlay. Main objects occupy about 45-55% of canvas.

Color palette: mostly black line art, sparse muted blue/gray for archive shelves/door hints, tiny restrained orange accent on notice board pin only. No red alarm color.

Text: no readable text, no pseudo-text, no letters, no numbers. All signs and papers must be blank.

Constraints: same recurring Xiaohei look: small solid black blob creature, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan, not cute. Archivist must look careful and dignified, not evil or hostile. Visual should imply careful recordkeeping and fear of losing files, not villainy. Leave clean blank areas for local text overlay. Avoid speech bubbles, caption boxes, UI screenshots, dense city background, control tower, logos, watermarks, fake text, malformed letters.
