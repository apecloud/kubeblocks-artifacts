# Panel 2 v4 Prompt

Use case: comic-panel-base
Asset type: 16:9 comic panel base for a four-panel series

Primary request: Generate a text-free base illustration for Episode 3 Panel 2 of a minimalist Xiaohei-style comic. Scene: the same dignified database archive/library entrance from Panel 1, now with the careful archivist anxiously boarding or bracing the archive doors with a few simple wooden planks to express fear of an upgrade. The archivist looks worried and protective, not angry, villainous, or obstructive. Outside the door, a finance analyst character stands with a small report folder or spreadsheet papers, politely stressed and needing numbers by morning. A small solid-black Xiaohei creature with white dot eyes may observe from the side, still not doing the main action. No control tower, no KubeBlocks, no logo.

Style/medium: pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly pen lines, absurd editorial sketch style inspired by Ian Xiaohei illustrations. Lots of empty white space.

Composition/framing: horizontal 16:9. Archive entrance center-left with partially boarded/braced doors. Archivist near the door with a protective posture. Finance analyst on the right or foreground outside, holding papers. Leave open whitespace above/right for local dialogue overlay and a small area near the analyst for a second line. Main objects occupy 45-55% of canvas.

Color palette: mostly black line art, sparse muted blue/gray for archive interior/doors, very restrained tan/brown on wooden planks only. No red alarm color, no disaster lighting.

Text: no readable text, no pseudo-text, no letters, no numbers. All papers, planks, signs, and folders must be blank.

Constraints: same recurring Xiaohei look if included: small solid black blob creature, white dot eyes, thin legs, deadpan, not cute. Archivist must look careful and worried about losing records, not evil. Finance analyst only represents business pressure and should not dominate the panel. Show upgrade fear and business stakes, not a system outage. Avoid speech bubbles, caption boxes, UI screenshots, dense city background, control tower, logos, watermarks, fake text, malformed letters.
