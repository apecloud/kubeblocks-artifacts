## Base Image Prompt

Generate a clean text-free base illustration for Panel 1 of an English 4-panel comic titled `One Operator, Seven Scripts`. Scene: Xiaohei, the only overworked database facility operator, is physically bracing a tall, shaky tower made of many blank script pages and folders. The joke is that Xiaohei says this is organized, but the image clearly shows manual script debt and fragile chaos.

Xiaohei is a small solid-black absurd creature with two white dot eyes, tiny thin legs, uneven hand-drawn body, no mouth, blank tired serious expression; not cute, not heroic. Xiaohei should lean into the paper/script tower with both arms as if preventing it from falling.

The script tower should be tall, messy, and unstable: many blank loose papers, binders, curls, small cables, and wobble marks. It should feel like many separate scripts for many databases, but no readable text anywhere. Leave a large clean blank area on the right side for later local text overlay.

Background: pure white with very sparse distant Database City facility silhouettes, secondary and quiet. Add only tiny red non-text wobble or alert accents if useful. No KubeBlocks control tower, no product UI, no Kubernetes logo, no control center, no rescue signal, no branded intervention. This panel must show the old painful state before the product reveal.

Ian Xiaohei style: minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly thin pen lines, lots of whitespace, clean absurd product-sketch feeling. 16:9 horizontal. Keep at least 35% blank white space.

Do not draw readable text, pseudo-text, labels, UI, speech bubbles, dialogue balloons, ordinary text boxes, caption cards, placards, or title text. All final text will be overlaid later.

Avoid formal architecture diagrams, dashboards, gradients, shadows, paper texture, commercial vector style, cute mascot poster, children's illustration, dense city background, and any malformed text.
