# Prompt

## Style Baseline

16:9 horizontal Chinese Xiaohei-style comic panel illustration. Pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly pen lines, lots of empty white space. Xiaohei is a small solid-black absurd creature with white dot eyes, thin legs and tiny arms, blank serious expression, serious and deadpan, not cute. Sparse red/orange/blue marks only. No realistic UI, no logos, no watermark, no gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no PPT infographic.

For text accuracy, each generated panel requested no readable text. Chinese was added afterward during local post-processing.

Series convention after v2 review: character dialogue and narration use unframed handwritten text. Keep borders only when the object itself is a screen, sign, label plate, or status plaque.

## Panel 1

3:00 AM in a quiet room. Xiaohei is jolted awake sitting upright in bed with the blanket over its legs, holding a vibrating phone. Through the window is a simplified database city at night: archive building smoking, delivery station in chaos, distant railway jammed. Clear silhouettes, no logos. Blank phone alert and speech bubble spaces.

## Panel 2

Xiaohei runs through the database city at night, wearing a blanket like a cape, holding a collapsing stack of old scripts. A small square-headed platform robot holds a blank sign, representing the platform world's default stateless bias. Database residents protest with blank placards. Keep the scene simple.

## Panel 3

A plain, calm KubeBlocks control tower / operations console appears in the middle. It is not a person and not a hero. Messy streams from worried database residents are calmly routed into four orderly lanes. Xiaohei watches in exhausted disbelief.

## Panel 4

Morning meeting. A simple square manager asks a question. Xiaohei points toward a calm operations control tower in the background. Database city is peaceful, residents sleeping, tower has one steady light. Blank counter plaque bottom right.
