# Prompt

## Style Baseline

16:9 horizontal Chinese Xiaohei-style comic panel illustration. Pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly pen lines, lots of empty white space. Xiaohei is a small solid-black absurd creature with white dot eyes, thin legs and tiny arms, blank serious expression, serious and deadpan, not cute. Sparse red/orange/blue marks only. No realistic UI, no logos, no watermark, no gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no PPT infographic.

For text accuracy, each generated panel requested no readable text. Chinese was added afterward during local post-processing.

Series convention after v3 review: all readable text should be black and unframed by default. Do not use red/orange/blue text to identify speakers. Avoid ordinary speech bubbles, text boxes, placards, signs, and label plates. Color is reserved for rare non-text accents such as alert marks, motion strokes, routing lines, and tower lights.

## Panel 1

3:00 AM in a quiet room. Xiaohei is jolted awake sitting upright in bed with the blanket over its legs, holding a vibrating phone. Through the window is a simplified database city at night: archive building smoking, delivery station in chaos, distant railway jammed. Clear silhouettes, no logos. No speech bubbles, alert boxes, placards, or signs.

## Panel 2

Xiaohei runs through the database city at night, wearing a blanket like a cape, holding a collapsing stack of old scripts. A small square-headed platform robot reads a small clipboard, representing the platform world's default stateless bias. Database residents protest with gestures only. No blank signs or placards.

## Panel 3

A plain, calm KubeBlocks control tower / operations console appears in the middle. It is not a person and not a hero. Messy streams from worried database residents are calmly routed into four orderly lanes without label plates. Xiaohei watches in exhausted disbelief.

## Panel 4

Morning meeting. A simple square manager asks a question. Xiaohei points toward a calm operations control tower in the background. Database city is peaceful, residents sleeping, tower has one steady light. No speech bubbles, status plaques, or counter boxes.
